# Changelog

All notable changes to ImmoManager Pro will be documented in this file.

## Unreleased — 2026-10-14 everyday workflow

- **Forms keep what was typed.** Escape, the close button, Cancel or a backdrop click on an edited form ask "Ungespeicherte Änderungen verwerfen?" first (a second Escape returns to editing); untouched forms close at once. A click only closes on the backdrop when it also started there, so selecting text and releasing the mouse outside no longer ends the form. Forms open in their first field instead of the close button, and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter saves from any field, multi-line notes included.
- **Tables remember their settings** per list in this browser: sorting, rows per page and hidden columns survive reloads and navigation (unusable stored values are ignored; without browser storage the table works as before). The table search also finds what the table shows: status labels ("Bezahlt") and dates as written ("14.10.2026").
- **Table CSV opens in German spreadsheets in columns**: semicolon separated with CRLF lines like the server exports, decimal comma for amounts and numbers, and cells starting like a formula written as text.
- **Global search (Ctrl+K) finds pages**: typing "mahn" or "uebergabe" offers Mahnwesen or Übergabeprotokolle (umlauts optional) above the records, Enter opens the first entry, and records of an earlier query are not opened while the new one loads. Records opened from the search are listed under "Zuletzt geöffnet" for an empty query (per user, in this browser only, with "Verlauf leeren").
- **Quick actions in the search**: "buchung" also offers "Neue Buchung" (likewise task, invoice, contact, maintenance case, appointment, receivable, unit, lead and the contract wizard), only for roles that may change that area. The page opens with its New form, also when it is already open; the address drops the marker, so reloading or changing a filter does not open it again.
- **Keyboard**: "/" opens the search, Alt+N opens the New form of the page's list, "?" (or the keyboard button in the top bar) shows all shortcuts. None of them fire while typing or inside a dialog.
- **Favourites**: the star next to the page name pins any page beyond the six everyday ones to a "Favoriten" section at the top of the sidebar (per user, in this browser only).
- **Several rows at once**: lists with actions get a selection column (a row, Shift+click for a range, the page, or all matches of the current search). Tasks can be completed, reopened or deleted together; contacts, appointments, leads, viewings, maintenance cases and the generic lists (categories, tax rates, insurances) can be deleted together after one confirmation. Every row goes through its normal request, so rights and deletion guards still apply per row: a protected record (for example a contact that is a service-contract provider) is reported with the server's reason and stays selected, the others are done. Rows hidden by the search are never acted on; read-only roles see no selection.
- **"Speichern & weiteren"** in new bookings, tasks, contacts, appointments, units, invoices, leads, meters and meter readings: the form stays open, keeps what a series shares (account, category, property and date of bookings; date and reader of readings; …), clears the rest and continues in the first cleared required field (Ctrl+Shift+Enter does the same).
- **Lists keep their state**: search, column filters and page survive opening a record and coming back (per browser tab). Lists no longer disappear behind "Laden…" while they reload after a change, so the table, a selection's result and an open series form stay in place.
- Receivable and notification-template forms are titled "Forderung erstellen" / "Vorlage erstellen" instead of a bare "Erstellen".
- The browser tab names the current page ("Mahnwesen · ImmoManager Pro"). Sign-in fields are labelled for screen readers and password managers.

## Unreleased — 2026-10-11

- **Invoice review (Rechnungsprüfung)** (migration `c9f4a2e7b1d5` after `5e8b2d4f7a19`, snapshot format 5; docs/INVOICE_REVIEW_20261011.md): a review beside the existing invoices, no second invoice model and no ledger of its own. Receipt with date and original document (SHA-256 at receipt, changes are shown); supplier + invoice number is the duplicate key (refused without a reason), same supplier/amount/near date is a warning. Factual check against the existing maintenance order link (over order or change order), the service-contract bill (tariff vs. bill, overlap with an already reviewed bill) or a free cost assignment; arithmetic check net + VAT = gross per rate and the § 14 UStG checklist (§ 33 UStDV for small invoices) as findings, never as automatic rejection. Approve, approve partially, reject or query the supplier with a reason; configurable four-eyes limit (owner/manager, never the checker). Append-only history (database trigger refuses UPDATE; invoices with history are not deleted). Payments stay `invoice_payments`; paid/partially paid/overpaid and Skonto are derived; payment proposals list only approved invoices (no bank file). Disputes stay open until a credit note, an agreed partial payment or a write-off settles them. Approved amounts feed project figures and service-contract reconciliation/transfer exactly once, a rejected invoice nowhere; a recoverable approved invoice enters the utility billing once. Roles: bookkeeping arithmetic/decision within the limit/payments/disputes, technicians the factual check of maintenance-order invoices only, owner/manager everything; rights re-checked until the commit. Portfolio scope everywhere (404 for foreign invoices). UI: Finanzen → Rechnungsprüfung (inbox with status/due/Skonto/property filters and server-side paging, review view with the document beside checklist and history, payment proposals), de/en/es, usable at 360 px. Legacy invoices keep working unchanged ("not reviewed"); after a decision the reviewed facts of an invoice can no longer be changed through `/invoices`.
- **Dunning (Mahnwesen) from claim to letter, payment and result** (migration `c9e4a7d2f1b6` after `c9f4a2e7b1d5`, snapshot format 6): runs per contract or per portfolio with a stored-nothing preview (claims, stage, fee, interest, what is excluded and why), then draft letters per contract queued in a durable outbox. Four configurable stages (Zahlungserinnerung, 1. and 2. Mahnung, letzte Mahnung) with days, fee, payment period and interest per stage. Default interest by § 288 I BGB (base rate + 5 points, base rate periods entered by hand, never assumed), exact Decimal per day/365, rounded once per claim; never on fees or interest. Payments are applied oldest claim first, so partial and late payments (also after the run) reduce the claim, change the draft (409 until recalculated) and stop the next stage. Excluded while pending: utility back-payments with an open objection, disputed claims, claims of cases written off or handed over; manual receivables are listed but not dunned. Dunning blocks per contract with reason and until-date. Finalizing (confirmed, idempotent per command key, rights re-checked until the commit) books fee and interest as receivables and archives the PDF as an immutable original linked to the receivables it covers; the outbox then offers print/download or an unsent e-mail draft (`.eml`) and a "sent" note — nothing is sent by the application. Results (paid, partially paid, written off, handed over) with date and reference. Replies attach to a case only by an explicit reference (Aktenzeichen), never by name; anything else goes to a review list; case timelines are paged without truncation. The daily escalation job proposes due stages once each (occurrence ledger) and never creates letters. Portfolio boundary on every list, file, PDF and outbox entry; bookkeeping may run dunning, technicians may not. Snapshot format 5, DSGVO export includes the dunning file. UI: Finanzen → "Mahnwesen" (cases, run, outbox, replies, settings) and a case file per contract, de/en/es, usable at 360 px. See [dunning](docs/DUNNING_20261011.md).
- **Deposit file (Kautionsakte) from agreement to settlement** (migration `3c7e9a1d5f20` after `c9e4a7d2f1b6`, snapshot format 7): agreement with amount capped at three net cold rents (above only with an explicit reason), form (cash, transfer to a separate account, deposit account, guarantee, pledged savings book), up to three instalments due at the start and with the next two rents, and the account holding the money. Receipts are shares of the tenant's bookings (partial and mixed payments allowed); that share is no longer credited as rent, so tenant account, review list and unassigned payments count every amount once. Interest by configurable rate periods with exact arithmetic, rounded per calendar year (no statutory rate assumed). At move-out a settlement draft offers the tenant's defects of the finalized move-out protocol (amounts by hand), open receivables and rent arrears to offset and a retention while the utility statement is open; finalizing archives the PDF as an immutable original and creates the payout booking or the remaining-claim receivable once per command key (offset bookings, settled receivables); a later release of the retention is its own settlement with its own original. Guarantee and savings book: no booking, only provided/released with date and proof. Database triggers keep settled files unchanged; bookings and receivables of a file cannot be changed or deleted. Bookkeeping manages deposits, technicians do not; portfolio boundary throughout. Snapshot format 5; the DSGVO export includes the new data. UI: deposit list page and a deposit file from every contract row and party contract card (de/en/es, usable at 360 px). See [deposits](docs/DEPOSITS_20261011.md).

## Unreleased — 2026-10-08

- **Maintenance case as project file** (migration `b6d4f1a8c2e7` after `f3b9c1d7e2a5`): work packages with phases and milestones, finish-to-start dependencies that refuse direct and indirect cycles (the message names the cycle), appointments as calendar events, craftsmen from the shared address book, quotes → orders (one per accepted quote, amounts frozen) → change orders (count once approved) → the existing invoices (each billed against at most one order) → payments as allocated outgoing bookings (`invoice_payments`, net of reversals, never counted twice). Budget, ordered, invoiced and paid are derived in exact cents; the file books nothing itself. Status workflow with history (also for the existing `PUT`/`PATCH`) and completion gates. Acceptance/inspection protocols with defects and photos are finalized into a PDF archived as an immutable original in one transaction; final rows cannot change (database trigger, integrity check against the original). Technicians plan, record quotes and change orders and finalize protocols but do not award or cancel orders, decide change orders or handle invoices; bookkeeping links invoices and payments. Portfolio boundary through the case; snapshots include the new tables; the existing `/maintenance` API is unchanged. New project view `/maintenance/:id` with seven tabs (de/en/es, usable at 360 px). See [maintenance projects](docs/MAINTENANCE_PROJECTS_20261008.md).
- **Handover protocol (Übergabeprotokoll) at move-in and move-out** (migration `a4d8e2f6c1b9` after `b6d4f1a8c2e7`): per contract a draft with rooms and their condition, defects (room, responsible party, agreement, due date, photos), keys (handed over/returned, missing count) and readings of the unit's meters, prefilled from the unit's last protocol or room count; photos as protected uploads within the portfolio boundary. Checking and finalizing render a PDF (photos embedded) that is archived as an immutable original in the same transaction that records the readings for the utility billing (an equal reading of the day is attached, a different one refused); database triggers then refuse changes, only a defect's resolution stays editable. Corrections are new originals that take over the readings; the earlier original stays byte for byte. Entry from every contract row and contract card in the party record, with the tenant change (previous tenant's move-out, next tenant's move-in, Wohnungsgeberbestätigung); no tenant-change letter templates exist. Snapshots and the DSGVO export include the new data. See [handover protocol](docs/HANDOVER_PROTOCOL_20261008.md).
- **Property service and energy contracts (Objektverträge)** (migration `5e8b2d4f7a19` after `a4d8e2f6c1b9`): electricity, gas, district heating, water, waste, cleaning, caretaker, elevator and other contracts with a provider from the shared address book, contract/customer numbers and several locations (properties, units, meters, metering point, cost weight). Tariff history by date (base price, unit prices with unit and up to six decimals, price guarantee, planned instalments). Terms with minimum term, fixed or open-ended renewal and notice periods computed by §§ 187/188 BGB (month ends, leap years); recorded cancellations take effect at the earliest possible end unless marked extraordinary. Expectation (instalments), bills (existing invoices, settlements credit the instalments) and payments (bookings, never beyond the booking, no tenant money) are kept apart and counted once; recoverable bills enter utility billing as existing cost items, prorated by service days, split by location weights and the recoverable share, once per billing period (also across corrections). A daily durable job (06:30 Europe/Berlin) creates one task and one notification per notice deadline, price-guarantee end and contract end. A contract is visible through any visible location; only accounts that see every location change it. Snapshot format 4. UI: Portfolio → "Objektverträge" with list, deadlines and a dossier (master data, locations, tariffs, deadlines, expectation/bills/payments, documents) in de/en/es, usable at 360 px. See [service contracts](docs/SERVICE_CONTRACTS_20261008.md).
- **Explicit upgrade, no hidden schema change.** The normal start initialises an empty database (schema plus Alembic stamp) and refuses every other database that is not at the Alembic head, with the command to run; the in-memory fallback never hides this. `python -m backend.upgrade` (Windows package: `ImmoManager-Pro.exe upgrade`; `--check`, `--no-backup`) takes a verified full backup first and then migrates, including the adoption of unversioned desktop databases. The desktop launcher (`python -m backend`, `.exe`, `start.bat`, Windows service), the Docker entrypoint, `scripts/update.sh` and the in-app updater run this step; `AUTO_MIGRATE` is ignored. A binary restore of an older `.db` backup is upgraded explicitly after its safety copy (newer or foreign databases are refused before anything is replaced); the table-by-table `ensure_*` patches are gone.
- **Integration secrets encrypted at rest** (AES-256-GCM, key id in every value, field-bound): key file `<DATA_DIR>/secrets/keyring.json` (owner-only, created on first use) or `SECRET_KEYS`; existing plaintext values are encrypted on the first load without loss; a missing key locks changes with a clear message instead of resetting; the API only ever shows `***`; `python -m backend.ops rotate-key` rotates and re-seals.
- **Full backups**: one ZIP archive with the database (SQLite online backup; PostgreSQL `pg_dump` from one exported snapshot, refused clearly without `pg_dump`), all uploads, integration settings and journal, `.env` and the key file (optionally sealed with `BACKUP_PASSPHRASE`), a manifest with SHA-256 per file, row counts and app/Alembic versions, and a `.sha256` file. Daily at 01:30 and a monthly isolated restore probe on the 1st at 03:30 (Europe/Berlin) as durable jobs; retention 14 daily / 6 monthly / 3 pre-upgrade; optional verified copy to `BACKUP_SECOND_TARGET`; CLI `python -m backend.ops backup|verify|probe|restore|status`; restore only into a new directory. Operations overview `GET /api/v1/admin/operations` (installation scope only) and a "Betrieb" panel under Settings → System (de/en/es).
- **Reproducible dependencies**: `constraints.txt` pins the whole Python dependency tree (`pip install -r requirements.txt -c constraints.txt`), `cryptography` and `psycopg2-binary` pinned exactly, CI tools pinned; Docker base images pinned by tag and digest, `postgresql-client-16` in the app image, data volume `/app/data`; install scripts use `npm ci`.
- **Upgrade notes for operators**: Windows/desktop: nothing to do, the launcher upgrades with a backup before the start (watch the console once). Docker: rebuild the image; the entrypoint upgrades, data now persists in the `appdata` volume (uploads stayed in the container before). Source/server installations: stop, update, `pip install -r requirements.txt -c constraints.txt`, `python -m backend.upgrade`, start. Back up `secrets/keyring.json` (it is in every full backup; with a passphrase keep the passphrase separately). See [operations, backup and secrets](docs/OPERATIONS_BACKUP_SECRETS_20261008.md).
- Utility statements (migration `c3e8a1f5d9b7` after `b8e3d5f7a2c4`): a consumption key counts one medium in one unit of measure; meters in another unit are converted only by an exact factor once the key names its billing unit (MWh → kWh), otherwise the statement is refused with a clear message (gas m³ → kWh, heat cost allocator units). A replaced meter counts until its removal date and its successor from its installation date (final + initial reading). Contracts get dated occupants and the person key shares by person-days; advances follow every change of the rent history, a change mid-month by days. Statements show these sections.
- Issued statements never change: every status from finalized on (also disputed) refuses regeneration and edits, the status changes only through the workflow, the shown document and PDF are frozen at finalization (byte-identical PDFs), the stores refuse content changes, and snapshot imports are checked against the finalized version. Objections are recorded with date and reason; a correction is a new version that names the version it corrects, marks it as corrected when finalized and bills only the difference. See [the billing regression](docs/BILLING_REGRESSION_20261008.md).
